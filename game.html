The wheel picks a family — a push, a pass, a whip, a turn, or footwork — then deals you up to three things from it. Spin, flip a card, and you've got something to go work on.

Some cards are locked face-up and dimmed. That's a move you can't draw yet because you haven't learned what it's built on. Learn the thing it names and it unlocks.

Footwork counts twice. A footwork drill trains the footwork and the anchor underneath it — so on this side of the site, it scores double. That's not a trick; it's just true.
